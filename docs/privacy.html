Privacy Policy

New Journey: Calm Meditation · by FrequenC · Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Terms of Service

New Journey is built so that what you do in it stays on your phone. We do not ask for an account, we show no ads and we use no analytics or tracking.

Who we are

FrequenC, Putselaan 187d, 3072 CH Rotterdam, the Netherlands (romario@frequenc.nl). FrequenC is responsible for this app.

What stays on your phone

  • Journal entries and moods. Stored in a database that is encrypted on your phone (AES-256). The key is protected by Android Keystore, so the file can't be read off the device.
  • Meditation sessions, progress, favourites and settings. Stored only on your phone, to show your progress and remember your choices.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not take part in Android backups, so it is not copied to cloud backups either. When you uninstall the app, all of it is deleted.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether you have an active subscription, and keeps that answer on your phone. Google's own privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the timer while you meditate, playback controls, and reminders you turn on yourself.
  • Background playback: so the timer, music and bell keep going with the screen locked.
  • Start after restart: to restore the reminders you set after your phone restarts.
  • Internet: reserved for downloading new music in a future update. The current version plays only music that is included in the app.

Children

New Journey is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. You stay in full control: you can delete journal entries in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. Under the GDPR you may also contact us, or lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If the app starts to handle data differently, for example by downloading music online, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
